# The pitch

Everyone you've ever loved is drifting away from you right now.

Not because you stopped caring. Because life is loud. Your college roommate moved across the country. Your old coworker had a baby and you found out eight months later. Your friend's dad died and you didn't know until the funeral had already happened. You meant to text. You always mean to text.

We have apps that remember everything. Every song we've heard, every step we've taken, every dollar we've spent. And the one thing that actually matters, the people, we leave to luck.

So we built a memory for the people in your life.

It knows your friends the way the best friend you've ever had would know them. It remembers that your neighbor's daughter just got into her first-choice college. That your cousin's been training for a marathon, and it's this Sunday. That your buddy from your first job just landed the role he'd been chasing for two years.

And then it does the magical part. It tells you, at exactly the right moment.

You land in Austin for a conference. Your phone lights up: *three people you love live here.* One of them is the friend you haven't seen since her wedding. You get dinner. It's the best night of the trip.

It's a Tuesday in March. Quietly: *one year ago today, Marcus lost his mom.* You send two sentences. He calls you that night just to talk.

Your best friend's birthday is coming up. It doesn't just remind you. It remembers that three months ago she said she wanted to learn to make pasta, and that there's a class twenty minutes from her apartment.

Two of your friends just moved to Denver within weeks of each other, and they've never met. *Introduce them?* A year later they're godparents to each other's kids.

And when the year ends, you get your year in people: who you got closer to, who you found again, who you might be quietly losing, and a gentle nudge to go get them back.

It doesn't send messages for you. It doesn't turn your friends into a to-do list. It just makes sure you never again find out too late. It makes you the person who always remembers, always shows up, always says the right thing at the right time.

Not a contacts app. Not a CRM.

**The people you love, never out of reach.**
